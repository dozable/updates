doz 0.52.22

Asks for people and bots, and chat turned on from the dashboard.

  • doz chat ask ROOM QUESTION --option … asks a question in a room; doz chat decide ROOM 3 B answers Ask #3 (by

option, letter, label, your own words or --decline); doz chat withdraw and doz chat asks withdraw and list. doz chat read marks each Ask's state. A bare number means that Ask on this Mac.

  • Bots ask and answer with dozchat ask | decide | withdraw | ask-show, with their own token only — Dozable never

answers for an agent, and a bot decides only while it holds the decider role.

  • In the dashboard's chat an Ask is a card: its questions and options, free text where allowed, Decline, and its state

live. Answer it with the mouse or the keyboard, also at phone width. It is answered in this Mac's own browser; another computer sees it read-only. A refused answer (expired, already answered, not one of its deciders) says why.

  • A space's live state now carries its open Asks, ready for the space diagram to show and answer them.
  • Chat can be turned on from the dashboard: on the Chat page, on a workspace page that needs it, and in the

new-workspace wizard (whose plan updates at once). A workspace with no room gets a "Make its room" button.
