A workspace opens on its Home, and a Workspaces page.

  • A workspace's page now opens on Home: the drawing of the workspace, then cards for its state and run, who manages

it and who is working, the room's latest, recent activity, folders and network — each linking to its tab. Needs you comes first when something waits: what is left to set up, open Asks in its rooms, agents waiting on you. The room is its own tab now (#/w/NAME/room); links to a room keep working.

  • Workspaces (from the sidebar's heading): every workspace with its picture, as tall rows or a grid — the layout and

sort are remembered in this browser — filtered by state, sorted by state, name or last activity.

  • A workspace made without a space file gets a small tile, so the drawn ones stand out.
  • doz workspace ls --json gives each workspace's last activity.
