doz 0.60.7

Agents held on your Mac run at full speed again, and the space editor draws compact. Canary only.

  • Sessions Dozable holds on this Mac ran at a low priority (utility, mostly the efficiency cores) whenever the daemon was started at login, so every held agent's builds, tests and browser ran slower than the same agent in a terminal. The daemon's login items now run as interactive, sessions are started at a normal priority, and a login item written before is rewritten at the next daemon start. The menu bar app's Start at Login picks it up at your next login, or when you turn it off and on.
  • doz doctor warns when the daemon or a session on this Mac runs at that low priority.
  • The space editor is on the isometric engine 0.26.0: workspaces can be drawn compact (labels much larger, nothing overlapping after a drag), and feeds are drawn.
