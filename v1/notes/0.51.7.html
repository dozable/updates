Chat in the dashboard: reactions, edit, delete, attachments — and doz chat in doz --help (issue #455; chat loop 698, step 13). On canary for the owner to look at, with 0.51.6's page; not promoted until they have.

  • Attachments: by the clip, paste or drop; waiting files show before you send; a file over 20 MB is refused with the reason before anything is sent; pictures open in a gallery. A file can never run as the dashboard — anything but the four common picture types downloads.
  • Chat on other devices: serve.chat (set on this Mac only; default off). Off: another device gets no chat. read: it reads, and its composer is disabled with the reason. write: it may post, react, edit, delete and attach — and each such act is one line in the audit log, without the content. A device allowed to write acts as YOU in chat, the person your agents take instructions from; the setting and the manual say so.
  • doz chat is listed in doz --help with completions, and has a manual page: turning it on, participants and tokens, webhook bots, agents on other machines (and exactly what that opens), import, the page, serve.chat, what is not there yet.
