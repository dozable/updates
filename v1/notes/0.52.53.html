The manual now tells the story Dozable has become. It opens with what Dozable is — teams of AI coding agents in workspaces on your Mac, each unit of work recorded in a journal and the lessons you approve carried into the next — and Getting started takes you to a first workspace in four steps, naming up front the three things that catch a newcomer: the account, chat, and long commands. Its index (llms.txt, and doz docs) is grouped into sections with a written line for every page, and starts with two entry points for agents: one for an agent driving Dozable for a person, one for an agent working inside a workspace. Three new pages: Runs, the journal and lessons; Bring your own process, worked through end to end; and Tokens and identity. Workspaces gains a table of what each preset sets up and what it does not, and troubleshooting lists the common failures by name.
