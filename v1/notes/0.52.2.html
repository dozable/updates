doz 0.52.2

A workspace can have a manager: one of your bots, started from its identity. There are no screens yet.

  • doz workspace new … --manager BOT [--manager-isolation fenced|vm|unfenced], and doz workspace edit W --manager BOT | --no-manager.
  • doz workspace start W first checks that everything is set up, and lists any gaps with their fixes. It then posts the brief to the main room as you, and starts the manager: fenced on this Mac by default, or in a micro VM, or unfenced only when named (labelled "acts as you").
  • The manager holds only a placeholder for its chat token, never the real token.
  • doz workspace stop W ends the manager and keeps its conversation; starting again resumes it and does not post the brief twice.
  • For now a bot runs in one live workspace at a time.
