doz.sh, the recipe directory (issue #257; loop 627, step 17 — backlog 617.B6). Owner 2026-10-30: "1. Pages ; 2. as you recommend", then "yes go for all of this".

  • The catalogue's new home: doz looks for its signed recipe catalogue at https://doz.sh/v1/recipes.json (a GitHub Pages site behind Cloudflare), still signed with Dozable's update key and checked exactly as before. Releases already out keep asking the old address, where nothing is published, and carry on with the recipes they shipped with.
  • Names in the directory: doz.sh/VENDOR/PRODUCT[@MAJOR|@VERSION] means the same as VENDOR/PRODUCT everywhere a recipe is named (doz try doz.sh/anthropic/claude-code). Nothing is fetched to resolve a name; any other host is refused with what to do instead, and so is a digest after @.
  • Publishing: make publish-recipes writes the site — the catalogue, a plain v1/index.json for the website, the CNAME — and refuses the update feed's folder, a site holding the feed, or a CNAME naming another host. With PUSH=1 it waits for the new serial to be live.
  • Docs: docs/RECIPE-SIGNING.md explains why the catalogue is signed, how the key is used, that it is signed and not encrypted, and why the update key is reused; the manual's recipe pages and RECIPE-FORMAT say how to name a tool in doz.sh.
  • Tests: the catalogue end to end (32/32), community recipes (48/48 — three checks fixed for 669's STATE column), the naming and catalogue unit tests (77), and CI's full run.
