doz 0.52.11

Add a worker, and the isolation guide.

  • Add a worker (#/w/NAME/agents/new, from the Agents tab, the side panel or an agent's menu): its one task, its

rooms (always the main room), an anonymous worker or one from a bot, and where it runs, with a table comparing a micro VM, fenced, unfenced and (later) the cloud — what each sees of your files, network and keys. Every field is checked as you type, without making anything; "Show the command" gives the same worker as a doz command.

  • Unfenced is never chosen for you: picking it asks you to tick "It acts as you: your files, Keychain and network".
  • End a worker from its menu, confirming its name.
  • Adding and ending workers works only in this Mac's own browser; another computer sees the command instead.
  • The isolation guide (#/guide/isolation) explains each choice and opens from any isolation label on the page.
  • doz worker add … --dry-run shows what would be made, and the command, without making anything.
  • The Agents tab's list of ended workers no longer fails on their times.
