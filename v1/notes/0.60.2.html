doz 0.60.2

The New workspace wizard is rebuilt around templates: it opens on five ready-made workspaces — One agent, One agent in a Sandbox, Build & review, Fix-it team and Remotion video — each drawn on its card, with ⤢ for a full-page view; pick one and press Create and start. Custom is five short steps (Name, Manager, Workers, How they chat, Confirm) with a drawing of the workspace as you build it: the name and a new empty folder are filled in (workspace1, workspace2, …), the description is optional, a bot is made without leaving the wizard and its isolation is never asked again, only managers that fit are offered, the room has a step of its own, and the last step shows only what needs fixing. doz workspace templates lists the templates and doz workspace new --template ID makes one; doz workspace new with no name takes the next free one. "Sandbox" is the word everywhere you read (--isolation sandbox is accepted, vm still works). The swift-tests, mac-admin and research presets are retired. Fixed: a second "New bot" no longer leaves the wizard, and New workspace no longer reopens an old draft.
