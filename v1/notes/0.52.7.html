doz 0.52.7

A workspace's own page: the room comes first.

  • #/w/NAME opens the workspace on its main room, with the brief above it ("The work": fold, Edit, History);

#/w/NAME/room/LABEL opens another of its rooms. The sidebar and Home lead there.

  • The workspace's own record shows between the messages as quiet lines ("started the work", "edited the work"), never

posted to the room. Under each agent's name: its role, program and place; unfenced agents are marked "acts as you".

  • A side panel lists the agents with their isolation, the folders and the network.
  • Editing the brief after the workspace has started posts one message in the room saying so, as you; while it is still

setting up nothing is posted. doz workspace edit --if-brief refuses an edit if the brief changed meanwhile.

  • doz chat read ROOM --with-log shows the workspace's record among the messages.
  • The tabs are drawn as planned; only Room works so far.
  • Another browser can edit the brief only when its chat access allows writing, and can never start or stop anything.
