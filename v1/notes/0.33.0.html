doz 0.33.0

Anonymous usage statistics

  • Official builds now send anonymous usage statistics, at most once a day: counts and ranges about how Dozer is used, never names, paths, contents or anything inside a sandbox.
  • Setup tells you, and lets you turn them off.
  • doz telemetry show prints exactly what would be sent. DO_NOT_TRACK=1 or doz config set telemetry.send_anonymous_usage_stats false turns them off.
  • Builds from the source repository send nothing.
  • The privacy policy lists every field: https://dozersandbox.com/privacy

Stay in touch

  • doz signup, or the last step of setup, signs you up for release news, early access, or tips and tricks.
  • A confirmation email comes first, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
