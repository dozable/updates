doz 0.52.17

The permissions model: what agents may do, with limits you set, and why.

  • doz permissions show | grant | revoke | ceiling and doz why AGENT PERMISSION: grants say allow, ask or never for

an agent, a bot or a role, in one room, one workspace or everywhere; ceilings — this Mac's, a bot's, a workspace's, and Dozable's own — cap everything; any "never" always wins. doz why shows each step of a decision and which rule won. doz grant … and doz bot ceiling … work too.

  • Roles come from the workspace (manager, worker, member), plus decider, admin and two manager presets you can grant.

doz permissions show --roles prints the table.

  • Permissions constrain agents, never you: everything you do in a terminal or this Mac's dashboard works exactly as

before, whatever you set. No agent can drive Dozable yet, so show says the grants are not enforced yet; a request that would need an Ask is refused for now ("Asks come later").

  • A workspace's Can do tab and a bot's page show its permissions and ceiling (read-only for now).
  • A new exit code, 9, means "not permitted".
