doz 0.52.25

The space editor in the dashboard.

  • A workspace's drawing now opens its space editor, a page of its own (#/w/NAME/space) you can keep in a window or

tab of its own: the space drawn live, its outline and properties, the plan (what up would do and its problems as you type), the runs strip to replay a run, and open Asks you can answer from the drawing.

  • In this Mac's own browser you edit and save the space file. Text adds the YAML beside the drawing (the text editor

loads only then, keeping your place and undo). A save is refused if the file changed elsewhere ("Changed elsewhere" — reload to see it); saving never brings the space up. Leaving with unsaved changes asks first, and a restart of Dozable never reloads the page over them.

  • Watch opens a read-only, full-window live drawing (#/w/NAME/space/watch) for a second screen.
  • Another computer sees the drawing read-only (and the text when chat is shared with it), never saves; a phone shows the

drawing, fitted to the screen.

  • Nothing of the editor loads anywhere else in the dashboard.
