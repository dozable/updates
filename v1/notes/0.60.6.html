doz 0.60.6

Workspace files get their own name. Canary only.

  • doz now writes a workspace's file as NAME.workspace.yaml: doz workspace new --template, the dashboard's templates and the space editor's Export. Files called NAME.space.yaml keep working, forever. If both are beside each other for one name, the .workspace.yaml is read and doz says once that the other is ignored. An override follows its own file's name.
  • The file's contents are unchanged, including its doz: space/3.x line, and so is the doz space command.
  • canonical/workspace/ in the repository is now the one source of the canonical workspaces: the demo app, three of the engine's examples and the five templates. The templates doz carries are generated from it, and a test fails when they drift apart.
