The dashboard's new shell: workspaces come first.

  • A new sidebar: Home, Chat, Agents / Bots, your workspaces (each opens its room; unfold for its rooms and agents),

and This Mac, which holds every page from before (Sessions, Sandboxes, On this Mac, Loose agents, Templates, Images, Accounts & keys, Resources, Devices, Logs, Doctor, Setup, Settings, Debug).

  • Home lists your workspaces by state, waiting on you first, with who drives each, its manager ("acts as you" when

unfenced), who is working, its room and the next step. With none yet, it says what a workspace is and how many sandboxes and sessions are not in any workspace.

  • New pages: This Mac's overview, Loose agents, and Agents / Bots.
  • Old addresses keep working: #/overview opens This Mac › Sandboxes, #/sessions This Mac › Sessions, and a

session on this Mac moved from #/mac/NAME to #/mac/here/NAME.
