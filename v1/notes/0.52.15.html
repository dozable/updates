doz 0.52.15

Runs and their evidence archive; finishing closes a workspace's room; rooms can be archived and removed.

  • A workspace now records its runs: each start…finish is one run, and sleep and wake stay inside it. `doz workspace runs

W` lists them, and the workspace page has a Runs tab showing each run, its archived chat and its timesheet.

  • Finishing a workspace archives the run first: the room's messages for that run (any number), each agent's

conversations — on this Mac, fenced or not, and in a sandbox — their last screens, usage and a timesheet, with a manifest of checksums. The archive is private to you, kept in Dozable's store, and checked for keys and tokens; if it can't be written, nothing is removed and it can be written again later. --no-archive finishes without one.

  • Finishing now closes the workspace's room: its members can still read it, but new messages are refused until you

reopen it. Finish's plan says so before you confirm. An asleep workspace's room stays open.

  • doz chat room archive R and doz chat room rm R: a room a workspace owns can be archived (read-only, copied first)

and then removed for good, after you type its name; a tombstone records when, from which run, and where its archive is. --loose does the same for a room no workspace owns. From the Runs tab in this Mac's own browser too.

  • doz sessions @mac --forget S --discard-transcripts removes a session's conversations once they are safely

archived — only files unchanged since, never anything else.
