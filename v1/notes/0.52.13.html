doz 0.52.13

Workspace-wide views: Can do, Network & secrets, Folders & points, Activity.

  • A workspace's Network & secrets tab answers "what can it reach?" for every agent: its network preset and extra sites,

what was refused lately (allow a site for one agent, or for all that can be limited), this Mac's ports, forwarded ports, the secrets held for each agent by name and destination — never a value — and the accounts in use.

  • Each agent's Can do tab lists what it can do today — its isolation, folders read-write or read-only, network,

secrets by name, and for the manager "talks in the room only" or "acts as you" — each marked live or from its next start. Permissions and limits are drawn as coming later.

  • Folders & points lists the files changed in the workspace's folders since it started (found without running any

program in those folders — not even git) and each sandbox's restore points, with Take.

  • Activity shows the workspace's log, newest first, a page at a time, with this dashboard's operations on its agents.
  • Allowing a site for a fenced session works only in this Mac's own browser; another computer sees held secrets only as

a count.

  • doz workspace show W --can-do | --network | --folders and doz workspace log W --limit N --before X.
  • Can do, the Network tab and doz sessions @mac --capabilities now agree that a session with an allowed site is

"custom".
