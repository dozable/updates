doz 0.52.39

doz workspace delete --bin-folders now moves to the Bin only the folders the workspace itself made (a new --new-folder folder, a space file's scratch folders), never your own: a folder it only references, one another workspace or a sandbox uses, one outside the workspaces folder, a .. path, or one that has become a link all stay where they are. The dry run, the dashboard's plan and the result list which folders go and which stay, each with why; the delete decides again just before each move. Workspaces made before this release keep all their folders (nothing recorded which they made). The dashboard's tick now reads "Also move the folders Dozable made for it to the Bin".
