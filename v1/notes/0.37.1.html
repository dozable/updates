doz upgrade trusts Dozable's Homebrew tap (issue #301). Owner 2026-10-20: "yes make doz trust the tap automatically".

  • Newer Homebrew loads a formula only from a tap you trust, and refused dozable/tap for a Mac that tapped it earlier or under its old name ("Refusing to load formula dozable/tap/doz-canary from untrusted tap dozable/tap"). doz upgrade and doz upgrade --channel … now run brew trust --tap dozable/tap first — only that tap (and the name it was tapped under), only when Homebrew has brew trust and the tap is not trusted yet — and say so in one line.
  • This takes effect from 0.37.1 on: upgrading FROM an earlier doz still needs brew trust dozable/tap once by hand.
  • The manual's "start completely fresh" answer now includes the trust step and the tap's full name.
  • Tests: make test-updates 59/59 (the fake Homebrew keeps a trusted list: trusted once, said, never twice); full make test 1128, 0 failures; docs-drift-check clean.
