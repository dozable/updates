The chat view, redone.

  • An Ask is a clear card: its title and why, a chip naming each question, the options as full-width lettered rows

("Something else" is the next letter), a quiet Decline and Answer; underneath, a pill with its state, when it expires, who decides and its number. Once decided it shows only what was chosen; declined, expired and withdrawn asks say so in a pill; an ask no one can decide says what happens next. On a phone the message is the card.

  • Messages: roomier bubbles, the time beside a message on a wide screen, the message tools in the name row (never over

the text; on a touch screen, tap a message to show them), day lines, a live dot.

  • The composer and its suggestions fit a phone (16 px text, so iOS does not zoom); the rooms list is roomier.
  • The people in a room are reachable at every width: a drawer below the widest screens, and the rooms list is a drawer

on a phone. The chat window gets all of it too.
